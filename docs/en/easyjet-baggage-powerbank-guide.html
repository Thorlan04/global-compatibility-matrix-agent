✅ Key Allowed Items & Guidelines

  • • One small cabin bag (45x36x20cm / 17.7x14.2x7.9in) per passenger is included for free.
  • • Larger cabin bags (56x45x25cm / 22.0x17.7x9.8in, max 15kg / 33lbs) are allowed with specific seat bookings or easyJet Plus.
  • • Power banks up to 100 Wh are generally permitted in cabin baggage.
  • • Power banks between 101-160 Wh require airline approval and are limited to two per passenger.

⚠️ Strict Restrictions & Pitfalls

  • • Bags exceeding specified dimensions will be subject to additional fees and may be placed in the hold.
  • • Power banks over 160 Wh are strictly forbidden on board.
  • • Power banks must always be carried in cabin baggage, never in checked luggage.
  • • Liquids in cabin baggage must adhere to 100ml container rules and fit in a clear, resealable bag.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is EasyJet's free cabin bag size?
EasyJet's free cabin bag (small cabin bag) must not exceed 45 x 36 x 20 cm (17.7 x 14.2 x 7.9 in) including handles and wheels, and must fit under the seat in front of you.
Can I bring a large carry-on bag with EasyJet?
Yes, but only if you've booked an Up Front or Extra Legroom seat, are an easyJet Plus cardholder, or have a FLEXI fare. The maximum size is 56 x 45 x 25 cm (22.0 x 17.7 x 9.8 in) and it must weigh no more than 15 kg (33 lbs).
Are power banks allowed on EasyJet flights?
Yes, power banks up to 100 Wh are generally allowed in your cabin baggage. Those between 101-160 Wh require airline approval and are limited to two per passenger. Power banks over 160 Wh are forbidden.
