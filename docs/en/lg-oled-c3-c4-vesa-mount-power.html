❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What size screws does the LG OLED C3/C4 use for wall mounting?
All LG OLED C3 and C4 sizes (42, 48, 55, 65, 77, 83) use M6 screws. Thread depth should be between 12 mm and 16 mm including bracket thickness.
What is the VESA pattern for the 55-inch and 65-inch LG OLED C3/C4?
Both the 55" and 65" models use a 300 x 200 mm VESA pattern located in the lower third of the rear casing.
Are the VESA holes centered on LG OLED TVs?
No! On the LG OLED C-series, the VESA holes are positioned toward the bottom. Mount your wall plate slightly lower than with standard centered TVs.
