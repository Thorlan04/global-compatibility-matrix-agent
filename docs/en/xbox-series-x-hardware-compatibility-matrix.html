❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I use any external SSD with the Xbox Series X?
You can use USB 3.1 external SSDs for storing and playing Xbox One, Xbox 360, and Original Xbox games. For Xbox Series X|S optimized games, you must use the proprietary Seagate Storage Expansion Card.
What kind of HDMI cable do I need for 4K 120Hz?
You need an Ultra High Speed HDMI cable (HDMI 2.1 certified) to achieve 4K at 120Hz. The console includes one.
Can the Xbox Series X play 8K content?
Yes, the Xbox Series X supports 8K output at 60Hz, though 8K gaming is not currently a primary focus.
