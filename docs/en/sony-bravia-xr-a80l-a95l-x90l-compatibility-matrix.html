❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What VESA mount size do Sony Bravia XR A80L/A95L/X90L TVs use?
All listed Sony Bravia XR models use a VESA mounting pattern of 300x300mm (11.8 x 11.8 inches).
What type of screws are needed for VESA mounting?
You will need M6 screws, typically 10-12mm in length, for secure attachment to a VESA mount.
Can I fit a soundbar under these TVs?
Yes, the A80L and A95L models feature multi-position stands that allow for increased soundbar clearance. For the X90L, a compatible soundbar bracket or a low-profile soundbar is recommended to ensure proper fit.
