✅ Key Allowed Items & Guidelines

  • • Always refer to your specific TV model's user manual for the most accurate VESA specifications and screw length recommendations.
  • • Ensure your chosen VESA mount has a weight capacity exceeding the TV's weight (including any attached accessories).
  • • Use M8 screws with a length of 10-12mm, ensuring they do not bottom out or are too short. Spacers may be required.
  • • Distribute weight evenly when mounting to prevent stress on the TV's mounting points.

⚠️ Strict Restrictions & Pitfalls

  • • Do NOT use screws longer than 13mm into the TV's VESA holes, as this can cause internal damage.
  • • Do NOT use VESA mounts that do not match the TV's specified pattern (e.g., 200x200, 400x300, 400x400).
  • • Do NOT exceed the maximum weight capacity of your chosen wall mount.
  • • Avoid overtightening screws, which can strip threads or crack the TV's casing.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What VESA pattern do Samsung Neo QLED and OLED TVs use?
Samsung Neo QLED and OLED TVs typically use VESA patterns such as 200x200mm (for some 55-inch models), 400x300mm (for 55-inch and 65-inch models), and 400x400mm (for 65-inch and 75-inch models). Always check your specific model's manual for exact details.
What size screws do I need for my Samsung TV VESA mount?
Most Samsung Neo QLED and OLED TVs require M8 screws. The recommended length is typically 10-12mm, but it's crucial to verify this in your TV's user manual to avoid damaging internal components. Do not insert screws deeper than 13mm.
How much does a 65-inch Samsung Neo QLED TV weigh?
A 65-inch Samsung Neo QLED TV (e.g., QN90C) typically weighs around 24.4 kg (53.8 lbs) without its stand. OLED models (e.g., S90C) of the same size might be slightly lighter, around 20.9 kg (46.1 lbs). Always check the exact weight for your model and ensure your wall mount can support it.
